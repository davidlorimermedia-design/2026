From complex problems to product direction.

My work often starts before an interface exists. I collaborate with stakeholders, engineers and subject-matter experts to understand operational challenges, uncover user needs and turn ambiguity into clear, actionable direction.

Strategy

Understand the whole problem

Research planning, stakeholder workshops, discovery, journey mapping and systems thinking establish what to build and why.

Design

Turn complexity into experiences

Guiding products through workflows, concepts, rapid prototyping and validation into detailed, production-ready interactions.

Systems

Design for reuse

Looking beyond individual features to establish modular patterns and shared capabilities that scale across squads and products.

Delivery

Connect design to engineering

Partnering with engineering to maximize component reuse, evaluate technical feasibility, and accelerate the path to code.
